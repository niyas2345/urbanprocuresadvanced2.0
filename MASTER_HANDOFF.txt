3. FINAL COMMERCIAL RULES — LOCKED
================================================================

These rules are authoritative.

---------------------------------------------------------------
A. CONTRACTOR / CLIENT
---------------------------------------------------------------

Urban Procures Service Charge payable by Contractor / Client:

AED 0

Contractor / Client must NOT be charged:

- 2.5%
- AED 500 minimum
- Vendor Service Charge
- manpower charge
- procurement commission

The Vendor Service Charge and manpower charge are Vendor-side obligations.

---------------------------------------------------------------
B. STANDARD VENDOR SERVICE CHARGE
---------------------------------------------------------------

For normal procurement / supply / subcontract awards:

Vendor Service Charge =

2.5% of applicable awarded value

OR

AED 500 minimum

WHICHEVER IS HIGHER.

Formula:

vendor_service_charge =
MAX(award_value × 0.025, AED 500)

Examples:

Award AED 10,000
2.5% = AED 250
Vendor Service Charge = AED 500

Award AED 100,000
2.5% = AED 2,500
Vendor Service Charge = AED 2,500

Always use:

SERVICE CHARGE

Never use:

COMMISSION

This calculation must be performed or independently validated SERVER-SIDE.

Do not trust client/browser-submitted charge calculations.

---------------------------------------------------------------
C. MANPOWER / LABOUR CHARGE — FINAL CONFIRMED RULE
---------------------------------------------------------------

For manpower / labour procurement:

URBAN PROCURES MANPOWER SERVICE CHARGE =
AED 1 PER PERSON PER HOUR

This is a VENDOR-SIDE charge.

The Contractor / Client must NOT be charged this amount.

Formula:

manpower_service_charge =
number_of_persons × hours_per_person × AED 1

If multiple days or shifts apply:

total_person_hours =
number_of_persons × hours_per_person_per_day × number_of_days

manpower_service_charge =
total_person_hours × AED 1

Examples:

1 person × 1 hour
= 1 person-hour
= AED 1

10 persons × 8 hours
= 80 person-hours
= AED 80

20 persons × 10 hours × 3 days
= 600 person-hours
= AED 600

The backend must treat manpower procurement as its own charge category.

Do NOT automatically apply the standard:

2.5% OR AED 500 minimum

on top of the SAME manpower chargeable component unless a separately
approved Urban Procures business rule explicitly requires both.

Default rule:

STANDARD PROCUREMENT
→ 2.5% or AED 500 minimum, whichever is higher.

MANPOWER PROCUREMENT
→ AED 1 per person per hour.

Do not double-charge the same component.

- authorized representative

- supporting documents

- platform rules

- quantities
- unit rates

- exclusions

- validity

- delivery requirements

---------------------------------------------------------------

identity/contact details masked.

- bypass identity masking
- technically identify the Client
- contact the Client outside the permitted Urban Procures process
- obtain competitor confidential information
- circumvent Urban Procures

7. STANDARD VENDOR SERVICE CHARGE
---------------------------------------------------------------

The Vendor expressly acknowledges and agrees that where STANDARD
PROCUREMENT business is awarded through Urban Procures, the applicable
Urban Procures SERVICE CHARGE is:

2.5% of the applicable awarded value

OR

AED 500 minimum

WHICHEVER IS HIGHER.

The Vendor expressly agrees that this is a Vendor-side obligation.

The Contractor / Client does NOT pay this Vendor Service Charge.

The Vendor's acceptance of these Terms includes acceptance of this
Service Charge obligation.

---------------------------------------------------------------
8. MANPOWER SERVICE CHARGE
---------------------------------------------------------------

Where the award concerns manpower/labour procurement, the Vendor
acknowledges and agrees to the Urban Procures manpower Service Charge of:

AED 1 PER PERSON PER HOUR

calculated on the total approved person-hours of the awarded manpower
requirement.

Formula:

number_of_persons × total_hours_per_person × AED 1

or where days apply:

number_of_persons × hours_per_person_per_day × number_of_days × AED 1

This is a Vendor-side obligation.

The Contractor / Client does NOT pay this manpower charge.

Do NOT combine this with the standard 2.5% / AED 500 rule for the same
manpower component unless explicitly instructed by an approved future
Urban Procures business rule.

---------------------------------------------------------------

A Vendor must not intentionally use an opportunity, introduction,
procurement requirement or contact obtained through Urban Procures to
avoid an applicable:

- Vendor Service Charge
- manpower Service Charge

Urban Procures process solely to avoid applicable platform obligations.

Do NOT invent:

- penalties
- punitive damages
- additional percentages
- fixed circumvention fines

unless separately approved by Urban Procures.

---------------------------------------------------------------
10. AWARD
---------------------------------------------------------------

Submission of a quotation does not constitute an award.

Shortlisting, discussion or clarification does not constitute an award.

An award exists only when an authorized Contractor / Client completes the
official Urban Procures award process.

---------------------------------------------------------------
11. IDENTITY RELEASE AFTER AWARD
---------------------------------------------------------------

Following a valid award, permitted contact information may be released

Non-awarded Vendors remain masked.

12. PERFORMANCE AFTER AWARD
---------------------------------------------------------------

The Vendor remains responsible for its supply/service obligations,

- quality
- workmanship

- warranty

- commercial terms
- obligations agreed with Contractor / Client

---------------------------------------------------------------

The Vendor is responsible for safeguarding account credentials and
authorized account activity.
